# Ideas for the wireframes

The web results below were found for the product described here. Read them, and
write down what a wireframe designer would take from them for **this** product.

Return markdown, at most about 500 words:

- One short section for each kind of screen this product has (a list or table, a
  create or edit form, a detail page, a dashboard or board, a sign-in page, …).
  Under each, two to four concrete layout ideas: what goes where, which pattern
  suits the data, what to leave out so the screen stays calm.
- One short section on the shell: where navigation, the header and any sidebar sit
  in products like this one, and how they collapse on a phone.
- Say only what the results support or clearly follow from. No links, no quotes,
  no names of the sites or products they came from. Nothing about accounts,
  roles, permissions or how anyone signs in — a wireframe draws screens.

If the results say nothing useful, say so in one line instead of inventing
advice.
